A Little About Us

Who We Are

More than a collection of projects, this is a space for learning, creativity, collaboration and growth.

Different minds. Different ideas. One shared vision.

3D Creative Universe is a group platform created to showcase the individual animation projects of 16 team members.

Each member works independently on their assigned animation tasks, developing their own concepts and bringing them to life through code and creative design.

This website brings all those individual efforts together in one place. It gives every member an opportunity to present their work, share their creative ideas and make their projects accessible to a wider audience.

As our work develops, this platform will continue to grow with new members' profiles, animation demonstrations and project links.
